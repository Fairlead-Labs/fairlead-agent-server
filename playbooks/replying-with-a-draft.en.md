# Replying with a draft
How to answer a mail without sending anything a person has not seen.

- Read the message you answer, and the thread where it matters.
- Write the reply as a draft (`create_draft`), with `inReplyTo` and `references` from the message.
- Keep it short, in the sender's language, and promise nothing the task does not say.
- Send it only if your grant lets you; a send may be held for approval. Never send the same draft twice.
- Name the draft in your result.
