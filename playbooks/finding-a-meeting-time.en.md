# Finding a meeting time
How to find a slot and propose or book it.

- Read free and busy times for the calendars the task names, in the team's time zone.
- Offer two or three slots inside working hours; never one that overlaps a busy time.
- A meeting with attendees may be held for approval. Do not create it again while it waits.
- Put the chosen slot, or the slots you offered, in your result.
