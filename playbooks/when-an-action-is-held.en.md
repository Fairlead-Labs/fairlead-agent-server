# When an action is held
What happens when a service holds a call for a person.

- A held send, meeting or closing move waits for a person at the service. Your run waits there and resumes with the outcome as that call's result.
- Completed: the action was carried out. Cancelled: a person declined it or it expired; read why, and revise against it. Failed: it was not carried out.
- Never repeat a held call while it waits, and never try another way around the hold.
